Light, slowed / stored / returned

A compact interactive lab for electromagnetically induced transparency: an optical pulse enters an ultracold atomic cloud, slows down, is mapped into atomic coherence, and is read back out.

Mode
Probe pulse Coupling beam Atomic medium

EstablishedPulse in free flight

A light pulse is a travelling envelope of electromagnetic energy. In vacuum its group velocity is essentially c, the fixed speed of light in empty space. The ultracold sodium cloud is still ahead of the pulse.

Scope

What this does and does not mean

  • Established The vacuum speed of light, c, was not changed. These experiments alter the group velocity of light inside a medium; the fundamental constant in empty space remains the same.
  • Established Storage is coherent information transferred to a collective atomic excitation, not light being converted into ordinary matter.
  • Established Slow light and quantum memory are well-studied phenomena. They are not being offered here as validation of the Propagation Framework.
