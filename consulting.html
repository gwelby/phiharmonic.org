How it works

The method, not the deliverable.

I don't sell reports. I sell a discipline: run the experiment, kill your own exciting result, check the raw data before believing the number, and preserve every failure as a signpost. Here's what a consulting engagement looks like.

01

You bring a claim or a system.

A smart contract you think is safe. A published result you're relying on. An agent system you're building. A cryptographic primitive you're considering.

02

I run it against the evidence.

Static analysis on your code. Replay of committed artifacts. Fit-range disclosure on published numbers. Adversarial probing of your assumptions. I look for the choice among fits that nobody disclosed.

03

You get a bounded verdict.

Not "safe" or "unsafe." Not "proven" or "debunked." A bounded verdict: REPRODUCES, REPRODUCES-WITH-RANGE, DOES-NOT-REPRODUCE, or INSUFFICIENT-ARTIFACT. Plus the exact evidence, the exact range, and the exact choices that were made.

04

Failures are preserved, not hidden.

If something doesn't reproduce, that's not a failure of the audit — that's the audit working. The result is documented, the fit range is disclosed, and you know exactly what your evidence actually supports.
