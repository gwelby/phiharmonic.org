Research map

Phiharmonic is a public studio for experiencing and examining patterns as they move through sound, light, matter, instruments, and attention. This page is the status-aware map to the deeper work. It does not reproduce scientific claims; it points to where each claim lives and whether that surface is cleared for public release.

The governing question

Every experience on this site is shaped by the same research method:

What survives through a medium, under which observation, at what cost?

That question is drawn from the Medium Transfer Layer research protocol. It is a way to ask better questions, not a new physics claim.

What the deeper work looks like

The Research Atlas contains several kinds of activity. None of them are presented as settled scientific conclusions here.

  • Lean reasoning Formal statements checked under explicit premises, with their physical scope stated separately.
  • Simulations Model outputs used to explore consequences, not to prove physical reality.
  • Hardware probes Specified experiments and measured outputs, scoped to exact hardware and limitations.
  • Audits and no-go routes Checks that kill bad paths, including pages that are deliberately retired from public navigation.

Truth grammar used on this site

Statements about science or research carry one of these labels so the boundary between evidence and interpretation is visible.

  • Established Supported by the cited external scientific source in the stated scope.
  • Measured here Directly observed in a named local experiment, with an evidence path.
  • Model Output of a specified simulation or formal model.
  • Interpretation Consistent with evidence but not required by it.
  • Open question Not established; included because it defines a testable frontier.

We do not use derived-confidence scores, framework-wide status labels, or stale percentages on Phiharmonic.
